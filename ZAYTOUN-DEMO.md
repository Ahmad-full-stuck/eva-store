# Zaytoun Group — interactive storefront preview

This is a dedicated storefront demo for Zaytoun Group, maintained on the isolated `zaytoun-demo` branch of the Eva Store repository. The original `main` branch is unchanged.

## Cloudflare Pages settings
- Production branch: `zaytoun-demo`
- Root directory: `zaytoun-demo`
- Framework preset: None
- Build command: leave blank
- Build output directory: `.`

The deployment root is isolated to `zaytoun-demo/` so the rest of the Eva Store repository is not published as the storefront.

## Interactive demo
- Arabic RTL, responsive storefront
- Product search and category filters
- Product variant choices and quantity selection
- Shopping cart and customer detail form
- Opens a prefilled WhatsApp inquiry and routes the chosen area to the relevant contact

## Important
This is a sales-demo preview, not a production commerce backend. Orders are not stored on a server; checkout opens WhatsApp and the customer must press Send. Prices and stock deliberately remain “upon request” until the business confirms real data. Product images load from public product/social pages; before production, confirm image permissions, product data, stock, prices, shipping rules and the correct order-routing numbers with Zaytoun Group.
